# Top-10 Market-Cap Companies — Employee Metrics Analysis

Analysis of the **10 largest public companies by market capitalisation** (June 2026),
modelling **1,000 key employees each** (10,000 total) across **three metrics**:
performance score, total compensation, and tenure — then comparing the companies and
writing up the findings.

> ⚠️ **Data honesty note.** The *company list* is real and sourced. The *per-employee
> records are synthetic* — no public dataset of any company's "1,000 most important
> employees" with personal pay/performance exists (and publishing real ones would breach
> privacy). The employee data is generated from distributions **calibrated to public,
> company-level characteristics**, so it is realistic in shape but is **not** data about
> real individuals. Fully reproducible (fixed seed). See the report for details.

## The 10 companies

NVIDIA · Apple · Alphabet (Google) · Microsoft · Amazon · TSMC · Broadcom ·
Saudi Aramco · Tesla · Meta Platforms.

## Headline findings

- **Pay and tenure move inversely** — the highest payers (Meta, NVIDIA, Alphabet) have
  the *shortest* tenures; the lowest payers (TSMC, Aramco) have the *longest*.
- **Market cap doesn't predict pay** — TSMC is #6 by cap but pays its senior cohort the
  least (~2.7× below Meta, which is #10). Geography dominates.
- **Performance scores barely separate the firms** (all within a ~5-point band); pay is
  driven by **level and employer, not by the individual rating** (comp↔performance
  `r ≈ 0.17`).
- The companies cluster into three archetypes: **high-pay/high-churn**,
  **high-pay/stable**, and **modest-pay/very-stable**.
